What the explanation means

The mask tells you what the trained model relied on for this prediction. It does not automatically prove a causal biological mechanism.

Formula lens

A mask explanation keeps the prediction similar while hiding as much of the graph as possible. The science begins when that mask is checked against perturbation, chemistry, pathway, or curated evidence.
